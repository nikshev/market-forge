## Notes

FR-008 is the criterion that makes this experiment honest rather than
persuasive. "Materially" is the whole question, and a default effect size would
be that judgement made by whoever wrote the module — after which any difference
can be described as material or not, depending on the number chosen.

FR-007's third value carries the same weight. Most confluence claims are "not
materially different", and a study that could only answer higher or lower would
answer one of them.
