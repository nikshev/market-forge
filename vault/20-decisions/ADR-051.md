## Context

EXP-013 asks for seven numbers over four arms. Three of them — root presence
rate, root horizon IQR, turn-type agreement — are defined in PRD §13A.12 as
properties of *one root*, over the perturbed members of its own lattice. EXP-013
wants them over a whole experiment, and that step needs a denominator nobody
wrote down.

The obvious one is wrong. Averaging a root's presence rate over every scored row
counts a row whose forward path never turns as a root that went missing. On the
first fixture built here — half the paths straight by construction — that alone
dragged the mean to 0.50 against the gate's 0.70, and the experiment reported
"roots are unstable" about roots that were stable in every member of every
lattice.

The same trap runs the other way for the horizon IQR, which the gate reads as a
*maximum*: a row with no root contributes no spread, and averaging that absence
in as zero halves the reported figure. There the error is in the direction that
lets an unstable set through.

Two further denominators had to be chosen: which arms are scored on a
time-to-turn error, and what happens to a call whose true path never turned.

## Decision

**The three stability figures are averaged over the rows that produced a root**,
and both counts are reported so the share of rows that turned stays recoverable.
It is simply a different number from the stability of the roots that exist.

**A classifier arm has no time-to-turn error and no extreme-price error**, and
they are absent rather than zero or borrowed. `direct_only` and `gmdh_direct`
output a probability and never name a time or a price. Filling those in would
judge the cheap arms on a promise they never made — and what the expensive arm
buys is exactly that promise, which is the question EXP-013 exists to answer.

**A call whose true forward path never turns is counted, not scored.** There is
no time to be wrong about, and treating the absence as a zero error would reward
an arm for calling turns on paths that never turn. Every call lands in exactly
one of three counts — compared, without truth, called without a root — and the
three add up to the calls.

**The ensemble is the mean of its two members, not their product.** A product is
zero wherever no root was promoted, which silences the ensemble on exactly the
rows where the classifier is the only thing speaking. An ensemble that discards
one of its two members is one member.

## Consequences

- **The verdict is about stability, not about how often the market turns.** Those
  are different findings and the report now carries both separately.
- **A reader comparing arms sees blanks in the derivative columns for the
  classifier arms.** That is the finding, not missing data.
- **`called_without_a_root` is a real number on the ensemble** and zero on the
  pure derivative arm, which is how the two differ.
- **The gate's own thresholds are unchanged.** [[ADR-041]]'s lattice and the
  §13A.12 defaults decide a single root; this ADR only decides how many roots go
  into one average.
