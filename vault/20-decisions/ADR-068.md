id: ADR-068
title: CVD is in base units, derived from the bars, until the bar stores the quote split
status: accepted
decides: [REQ-WP-088]
date: 2026-10-04
---

## Context

PRD §15.4 defines `delta_t = aggressive_buy_notional − aggressive_sell_notional` and `CVD_t = CVD_{t-1} + delta_t`,
in notional (quote currency), and `features/flow.py` follows it deliberately ("Notional rather than base
quantity, because the PRD says notional").

Nothing runs that code. Reading the bars table on 2026-10-04 showed that every bar already stores the split by
aggressor side, **in base units**: `aggressive_buy_base`, `aggressive_sell_base`, `delta_base`, with `trade_count`,
`first_trade_id`, `last_trade_id` and the quote **total** only. `resample.fold` sums them for every timeframe from
complete windows. The API does not expose them and no feature row exists.

## Decision

Produce the flow features **from the stored bars, in base units**, under names that say so (`delta_base`, `cvd_base`,
`normalized_delta_base`, `cvd_base_slope`, `cvd_base_acceleration`, `cvd_run`). No row is named `cvd` or
`delta_notional`; those registry entries keep describing the PRD's notional quantity and stay unproduced.

## Why

It puts a real CVD on the 15m chart now, from the whole history the bars hold, with no change to ingest, to the
bar schema or to the resample, and with continuity readable from the bars themselves. The alternatives were worse
now: a trade-level producer (a new coupling to the socket process and a second source of 15m flow), computing
notional as base times VWAP (invents a number), or adding the quote split to the bar (a schema change to the
central table, forward-only, no history).

## Consequences

- The CVD is in coins, not in dollars. A reader comparing it with a notional CVD from another tool will see
  different magnitudes and, over a trending price, a different shape; the pane is labelled "CVD (base)".
- This is a **deviation from PRD §15.4**, taken knowingly and recorded here.

## The way back

Store `aggressive_buy_quote` and `aggressive_sell_quote` in the bar (the bar builder already sees each trade's
notional), fold them in `resample`, evolve the bars table, then produce `delta_notional` and `cvd` per the PRD from
bars written after that change, and retire the `*_base` pane entry or keep both. This ADR is superseded when that
is done.
