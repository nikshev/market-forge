## Context

PRD §26.1 gives the message in full: header, venue and timeframe, price and
time, setup name, **score out of 100**, **model probability**, a channel block,
an order-flow block, a **derivatives** block, a **DeFi** block, invalidation,
research target, and an `[ OPEN CHART ]` button.

Of those, four cannot be produced today:

- **Score** comes from PRD §22.3's alert threshold and §43's alert ranker.
  Neither is built, and neither is this work package.
- **Model probability** would need a model. Principle IV forbids one before
  deterministic baselines exist and leakage tests pass.
- **Derivatives** is REQ-WP-013.
- **DeFi** is REQ-WP-014 and REQ-WP-015.

PRD §26.3's severity tiers — WATCH 60-74, ALERT 75-84, HIGH 85+ — are score
ranges. Without a score there is nothing to place in them.

## Decision

**Sections with no data are omitted from the message entirely**, not rendered
with placeholders, dashes or zeroes.

**No severity is assigned.** Confirmed candidates alert; nothing else does.
`INFO candidate — normally no Telegram` from §26.3 is honoured by the fact that
only confirmations reach the alerter at all.

## Consequences

- **A placeholder in a trading alert is worse than a gap.** "Score: —" beside
  "Score: 82/100" is a formatting difference; a reader scanning a dozen alerts
  reads the shape, not the field. A section that is absent is unmistakably
  absent.
- **The message will look thin against §26.1, and that is the report.** The
  distance between the two is exactly how much of the analysis stack is still
  missing. Same argument as [[ADR-009]] and returns, and [[ADR-013]] and
  divergence.
- **A severity invented here would be quoted as a severity.** Whatever mapped
  quality-score-to-HIGH would become the alert threshold, chosen in a message
  formatter rather than in the ranker PRD §43 specifies for it.
- **Adding a section later adds a block, not a redesign.** The renderer takes
  optional inputs; a derivatives block arrives with REQ-WP-013 as one more
  optional argument.
- Reversible; the omissions are conditional, not structural.
