## Context

The first honest replay found it. Running 200 bars of a declining channel
through the backtest produced exactly **one** candidate: it opened at bar 59,
confirmed at bar 62, and then held the machine for the remaining 138 bars.

The cause is not a bug in either component. FR-014 allows one candidate per
symbol and timeframe. PRD §21.2's lifecycle sends a confirmed candidate to
`ALERTED` or `RESOLVED`, and nothing in the codebase performs either move:

- `ALERTED` belongs to the alerting layer — REQ-WP-008, not built.
- `RESOLVED` needs an outcome definition. PRD §40 defines what counts as a win,
  a loss and a target hit; ADR-009 already deferred that whole area, because a
  return without the costs §41 rule 9 requires is a number that would be quoted
  without its caveat.

So a confirmed candidate can leave `CONFIRMED` only through `INVALIDATED` — a
quality collapse or a close beyond the outer tolerance. If price behaves, it
never leaves at all.

## Decision

**Leave it.** Backtest v1 does not invent a resolution rule.

The gap is recorded here and made visible in the report instead: a run that
opens one candidate and confirms it reports `candidates_opened=1`,
`confirmed=1`, and an empty `terminal_reasons` — which reads exactly as what
happened.

## Consequences

- **A long replay under a calm market reports almost nothing.** That is the
  honest reading of the current engine, not a defect in the replay. A backtest
  that quietly reopened candidates would report activity the live system would
  not have produced, and REQ-WP-010 exists to make replay match live.
- **Test fixtures must break the channel to exercise the lifecycle.** The
  fixture in `tests/unit/backtest/conftest.py` breaks price beyond the
  tolerance every 90 bars for exactly this reason, and says so. A fixture that
  did not would test the opening step and nothing else — and would pass a
  runner that dropped every later transition.
- **Inventing a resolution rule here would be strategy design in a replay
  harness.** Whatever rule was chosen — target reached, opposite boundary
  touched, N bars elapsed — would become the definition of a successful signal
  by accident, in the one component whose whole purpose is to not have opinions
  of its own.
- Reversible, and cheaply: resolution is a transition the machine already
  permits. What is missing is the rule, and the rule arrives with §40.

## Follow-up

REQ-WP-008 (alerting) supplies `ALERTED`. `RESOLVED` waits on an outcome
definition; that work is unscheduled, and this ADR is the record of why the
lifecycle is currently open-ended.
