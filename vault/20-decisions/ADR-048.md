## Context

[[ADR-009]] kept backtest v1's report to signal quality — counts, confirmation
rate, terminal reasons — and reported no returns at all. Its reasoning:

> A return computed without costs would be a lie with a number attached. §41
> rule 9 exists because gross returns flatter every strategy, and a backtest
> that reports 62% win rate before fees will be quoted as 62%. Reporting nothing
> is honest; reporting a gross figure with a caveat is not, because the caveat
> does not travel with the number.

It also named its own condition: returns "arrive with the outcome definitions of
§40 and the fill models of §25.4, together with the costs §41 rule 9 requires".
All three now exist.

Nine of the seventeen `REQ-EXP-*` experiments ask for expectancy, profit factor,
or "incremental value after costs". Without this they cannot be closed at all.

## Decision

**§25.5's economic metrics are computed, and a cost model is a required
argument.** `economic_report` refuses without one. That is ADR-009's reasoning
kept rather than dropped: a caveat does not travel with a number, and a required
argument does.

**The signal-quality report is unchanged.** `BacktestReport` still carries no
returns and still refuses the field names in `FORBIDDEN_ECONOMIC_FIELDS`. A run
that has not been given costs produces exactly what it produced before, and the
two reports stay separate — so a reader holding one is never holding a gross
figure that looks like a net one.

**Ambiguous outcomes are excluded from every economic metric and counted.**
§40's "never choose the favorable ordering" would otherwise be undone one step
later: an ambiguous trade counted as a win flatters this run, and counted as a
loss flatters the next configuration that avoids them.

## Consequences

- **ADR-009's restriction is lifted on its own terms**, not overridden. Its
  final line said the change would be "adding metrics to a report, not a second
  engine", and that is what happened.
- **A gross figure is now impossible to obtain by accident.** It takes
  constructing a `CostModel` of zeros, which is a visible decision at the call
  site.
- **`FORBIDDEN_ECONOMIC_FIELDS` still guards the quality report**, and the test
  that enforces it is unchanged. The two reports answer different questions and
  a merge would make the distinction depend on which fields happened to be
  populated.
- **The phase-2 fills remain unbuilt**, so every economic figure rests on a
  market fill — at the next open, or at the close with slippage. §25.4's
  trade-through and L2-aware models would change these numbers, and they are
  named as absent in `fills.py`.
- **Nothing here defines a strategy's stop or target.** They are supplied; an
  outcome resolver that invented either would be scoring its own choices.
