## Context

PRD §14.1 asks for VAH, VAL and a "value-area percentage configurable, default
70%". It does not say how the area is grown, and the answer is not implied by
the target: several rules produce a set holding 70% of volume, and they produce
different sets.

- **Expand from the POC, taking the larger adjacent bin each step.** The
  classic Market Profile construction.
- **Expand from the POC, taking bins in descending volume order.** Reaches 70%
  in fewer bins, but the result need not be contiguous — and a value area with
  a hole in it is not a range.
- **Take the narrowest contiguous window holding 70%.** Need not contain the
  POC at all, which makes VAH and VAL describe a region the market's most
  agreed price is outside of.

The differences are not academic on a bimodal profile, which is exactly the
shape that matters — two shelves with a gap between them.

## Decision

**Expand from the POC, taking the larger adjacent bin at each step**, until the
configured share is reached or the profile is exhausted.

Ties between the two neighbours go to the **lower** price, the same direction
the POC's own tie-break goes, so one rule covers both.

When the target cannot be reached by a proper subset, the whole profile is the
value area and **the result says so**.

## Consequences

- **The value area is contiguous and contains the POC, always.** Both are
  properties a reader assumes from the name; a construction that broke either
  would be surprising in a way nobody checks for.
- **The choice is recorded rather than implied by the code.** Someone comparing
  this profile to a charting package that expands by descending volume will get
  different numbers, and this ADR is what tells them why rather than sending
  them to read the loop.
- **The tie-break direction is arbitrary and stated.** Lower over higher has no
  justification beyond consistency with the POC's; what matters is that it is
  the same every run, so two builds of one trade set give one profile
  (Principle XI).
- **The "target not met" flag is small and load-bearing.** A value area equal to
  the whole profile looks like a wide market; it may instead mean the profile
  had four bins. The flag distinguishes them.
