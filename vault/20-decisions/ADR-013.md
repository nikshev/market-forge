## Context

PRD §15.4 lists five features under trade imbalance:

- delta per bar;
- CVD slope;
- **CVD-price divergence**;
- CVD acceleration;
- normalized delta / total volume.

Four have formulas or are one arithmetic step from the ones given. The third
does not. "Divergence" between two series is a family of definitions, and the
choices are not equivalent:

- **sign disagreement** — CVD rising while price falls, as a boolean;
- **normalized slope difference** — the gap between two standardized slopes,
  which needs a normalization the PRD does not specify;
- **correlation breakdown** — rolling correlation dropping below a threshold,
  which needs a window and a threshold;
- **swing-based** — price makes a higher high while CVD does not, which needs
  a swing definition (PRD §13A has one, for a different purpose).

Each gives a different number, and each would be reported under the same name.

## Decision

**Not built.** The four features with definitions ship; divergence does not.

## Consequences

- **A divergence feature invented here would become the definition.** Whatever
  was implemented would be registered as `cvd_price_divergence`, consumed by
  signals, and evaluated in backtests — and the choice made in an afternoon
  would be indistinguishable from one the PRD asked for.
- **The gap stays visible.** §15.4 lists five features and this package
  registers four; the difference is legible in the registry, which is exactly
  what PRD §19 makes it for. Filling it with a plausible formula would hide it.
  Same argument as [[ADR-009]] and returns.
- **The inputs are all here when someone defines it.** CVD, its slope and its
  acceleration are built; price series come from REQ-WP-005. Divergence is a
  function over things that exist, so adding it later adds a function, not a
  subsystem.
- Reversible at the cost of one registry entry.
