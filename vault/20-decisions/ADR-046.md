## Context

PRD §27.5 defines two views of a channel:

> `AS-SEEN-THEN`: immutable channel snapshot at selected time;
> `CURRENT REFIT`: channel calculated now over visible/current history.

`api/channels.py` serves both, and its refit is capped at the requested instant
— it cannot see past it, and its docstring says so twice. That is the right
default for an endpoint a chart calls, and it makes the two modes a comparison
of one information set: a model-drift check.

REQ-US-003 asks for something else. The researcher wants "the historical
snapshot of the channel that actually existed at that moment against the model's
**later** state", and a later state is one that has seen later bars. Without
them there is no repaint to expose, and §27.5's claim that the distinction
"directly exposes repaint-like differences" is not yet true of the code.

Constitution Principle I forbids look-ahead. This measurement is made of it.

## Decision

**The comparison refits over history up to a caller-supplied later instant**,
which may be after the instant being compared. Principle I governs what a
*feature* may see; here the hindsight is the subject of the measurement, and
hiding it would remove the only thing being measured.

Three things keep that from becoming a leak:

1. **The span is stated.** Every comparison carries `hindsight_ns`, the distance
   from the instant to the later one. Zero means the two channels saw one
   information set and the difference is the model's.
2. **The payload declares itself.** `research_only` is `True` on every
   comparison, in the object and over the wire.
3. **The signal path cannot reach it.** A test walks the signal, alerting, stop
   and extrema packages and asserts none of their import lines mentions this
   module — [[ADR-040]]'s device, because intent is not checkable and a fact
   about the source tree is.

`channels.py` is unchanged. Its refit still caps at the requested instant, and
this module lives beside it rather than inside it: a hindsight path inside the
module whose docstring promises there is none is how the promise stops being
true.

## Consequences

- **§27.5's claim becomes true of the code.** A researcher can measure how far a
  channel moves once the model sees what followed, which is what a repaint is.
- **The endpoint is easy to misuse and hard to misuse silently.** Anything built
  on it carries `research_only` and a non-zero hindsight span; a reader who
  ignores both has been told twice.
- **The import ban is checked over import lines, not the word "comparison"**,
  which is ordinary English and appears in these packages' prose. A check that
  failed on a docstring would be renamed away rather than fixed.
- **Nothing enforces that a future package joins that list.** The four named are
  today's signal path; a fifth would need adding by hand, the same open edge
  [[ADR-040]] carries.
