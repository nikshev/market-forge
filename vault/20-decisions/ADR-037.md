## Context

PRD §18.13 lists nine mapping fields, and two of them do not apply to every
asset:

- **bridge issuer** — "if relevant";
- **stablecoin family** — meaningless for ETH.

The obvious encoding is `str | None`, and `None` then carries two different
meanings at once:

- **not applicable** — native ETH has no bridge issuer, and never will;
- **unknown** — this bridged token has an issuer and nobody recorded it.

Downstream those are opposite facts. A consensus price that includes an asset
whose bridge issuer is *not applicable* is including a native token. One that
includes an asset whose issuer is *unknown* is including something nobody has
checked the provenance of — which, on a bridged stablecoin, is the difference
between a price and a rumour.

With one `None` for both, no consumer can tell, and the check that would have
caught it cannot be written.

## Decision

**Both fields are three-state.** A value, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, or `UNKNOWN`. There
is no default and no way to omit them: a representation registered without
stating which is refused.

The same encoding is used for any later §18.13 field with the same shape.

## Consequences

- **A provenance filter becomes possible.** "Every representation in this
  consensus has a known or inapplicable bridge issuer" is a checkable
  statement; with `None` it was not expressible.
- **Registration is more work, deliberately.** Someone adding WETH on Base has
  to decide whether its bridge issuer is unknown or does not apply, and that is
  the moment they still know. Asking later means asking someone who does not.
- **This is the third time the same instinct has paid here.** [[ADR-015]]'s
  registry gives no field a default, [[ADR-026]]'s z-score refuses rather than
  returning zero, and [[ADR-036]]'s depth refuses rather than returning a
  smaller number. Each is the same rule: a degenerate case gets its own answer,
  never a plausible one borrowed from a real case.
- **`UNKNOWN` is a legitimate state, not a failure.** A registry that only
  accepted complete knowledge would be empty. What it refuses is silence about
  which kind of gap this is.
