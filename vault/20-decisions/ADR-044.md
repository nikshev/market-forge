## Context

PRD §22.1 sets six feature groups with caps summing to 100, and then adds:

> "Missing family must not automatically equal zero; score should account for
> data availability with explicit confidence downgrade."

Two readings satisfy the first clause:

1. the family contributes zero and is flagged as missing;
2. the family leaves both the numerator and the denominator, and the score is
   normalized over the caps that were observable.

Reading 1 keeps the score on a fixed 0–100 axis, and a DeFi outage costs the
setup ten points. That number is then indistinguishable from a setup where the
DeFi evidence was genuinely against it — which is the conflation the sentence is
about, only moved one step downstream.

Reading 2 keeps the score meaning "how good is the evidence we have", and needs
a second number saying how much evidence that was.

## Decision

**Reading 2.** A missing family is excluded from the denominator, listed in
`missing`, and the `confidence` reports the share of §22.1's 100 points that any
family could speak to. A family present but scoring zero contributes zero and is
*not* listed as missing — that distinction is the whole point.

The confidence is separate from §22.2's data quality multiplier. They answer
different questions: the multiplier is about the freshness of the sources
present, the confidence about which families exist at all. Folding them together
would produce one number that means neither.

## Consequences

- **A score is always comparable against the threshold**, whatever was missing.
  Under reading 1 a 75 threshold would be unreachable during any DeFi outage
  without anyone deciding that.
- **A low-confidence score can outrank a complete one**, and the confidence is
  what tells a reader that. §43's `data_quality` factor is where a caller turns
  that into a ranking penalty if they want one — deliberately not here.
- **A partial score reads high.** Channel structure alone at 24 of 30 scores 80.
  That is the intended meaning — 80% of the evidence available was positive —
  and the confidence of 0.3 beside it is what stops it being read as a complete
  one.
- **The alternative stays available**: a caller who wants reading 1 supplies a
  zero contribution for the family. The engine does not choose it silently.
