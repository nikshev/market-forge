## Context

PRD §41 lists eleven anti-bias rules. All eleven are `hard_gated` requirements
in this repository, which validator rule R5 never waives — and all eleven have
sat at `draft` since extraction, because none of them had anything to attach
to.

REQ-WP-017's four deliverables — as-of joins, leakage asserts, labels, purged
chronological folds — are where most of them live. Not all of them.

## Decision

**Six rules are satisfied here and advance to `implemented`:**

- **1, no random train/test split** — folds are chronological and shuffling is
  refused.
- **3, no pivot requiring future bars unless availability is shifted to
  confirmation time** — turning-point labels carry REQ-WP-019's `known_at`,
  which *is* the confirmation time. The rule's escape clause is exactly what
  §13A.1 built.
- **4, no final daily high/low before daily close** — the join refuses a
  feature derived from a bar that had not closed at the as-of time. Generalized
  from "daily" to any timeframe, which is stricter, not looser.
- **7, no survivor-only universe without point-in-time listing history** — the
  universe is reconstructed at each instant.
- **8, no current top-volume list for a historical universe** — eligibility is
  computed from trailing data only.
- **10, optimize on train/validation; locked test untouched** — the test split
  refuses to be read until explicitly unlocked.

**Five stay at `draft`, and each for a reason that is not "later":**

- **2, no centered moving filters in live features.** REQ-NRT-D enforces it for
  the extrema engine ([[ADR-022]]) and nothing enforces it generally. Marking
  it implemented on the strength of one engine's guard would claim coverage
  that does not exist.
- **5, funding settlement** — needs REQ-WP-013.
- **6, corrected DEX state** — needs REQ-WP-014 and REQ-WP-015.
- **9, fees and slippage in economic evaluation** — [[ADR-009]] records why no
  economic metric exists to include them in.
- **11, store discarded experiment variants** — experiment tracking, which
  nothing here does. *(Superseded on 2026-09-09: [[ADR-054]] gives rule 11 a
  home. The registry and the reporting gate exist; the rule is enforced wherever
  a result is published through them, and nothing publishes through them yet,
  so `REQ-BIAS-011` sits at `specified` rather than `implemented`.)*

## Consequences

- **Six hard-gated rules become enforceable rather than aspirational.** Each
  gets a test that constructs the violation and asserts it is caught, which is
  what `hard_gated` was for.
- **Rule 4 is honoured more strictly than written.** The PRD says daily; the
  join refuses any unfinalized bar at any timeframe. A 15-minute bar read
  before it closed is the same defect at a different scale, and narrowing the
  check to daily would leave the common case open.
- **Rule 2's partial coverage is stated, not quietly counted.** This is the
  distinction that matters most in this ADR: a constraint enforced in one place
  and not in general is not satisfied, and the graph should not say it is.
- **The remaining five name what would close them**, so the next work package
  touching funding, DEX state, costs or experiments knows it inherits one.
