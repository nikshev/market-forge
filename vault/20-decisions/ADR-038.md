## Context

PRD §18.13's prohibition is one sentence: "Do not merge wrapped, bridged or
synthetic assets only by ticker."

It is easy to agree with and easy to violate, because ticker matching is what
every convenient shortcut reduces to. A lookup helper that takes `"USDC"` and
returns an asset has merged by ticker. A dictionary keyed on symbol has merged
by ticker. A fallback that resolves an unmapped representation "by name" has
merged by ticker, with an apology in a comment.

The failure is silent and expensive. Bridged USDC on a minor chain and native
USDC are different assets with different depeg risk; a consensus price across
both reports agreement that does not exist, and the number looks like every
other number in the system.

## Decision

**A representation declares its canonical asset explicitly**, and a
representation without that declaration is refused rather than resolved by any
other means.

**A lookup by ticker alone refuses when the ticker is ambiguous**, rather than
returning the first, the most confident, or the most recently registered.

**Identity is by chain id and contract address**, or by an explicit native
marker. Two representations differing in either are distinct, whatever they are
called.

## Consequences

- **Convenience is deliberately withheld.** `registry.by_ticker("USDC")` works
  only where exactly one asset uses that ticker, which on a real registry is
  rarely. Callers that want a specific token name it by chain and address,
  which is the only identification that means anything.
- **A dangling declaration is refused at registration**, not at read time. A
  representation pointing at an unregistered asset would otherwise surface much
  later, as a consensus price over an asset nobody defined.
- **Wrapper chains resolve, cycles refuse.** Wrapped-bridged tokens exist, so a
  wrapper pointing at another wrapper is legal; a cycle is a registry error and
  refusing names it rather than hanging.
- **This mirrors [[ADR-022]] and [[ADR-034]].** In both, the thing being used
  declares what it is and the system refuses what it cannot vouch for, rather
  than inferring from a shape that usually correlates. Inference is where the
  silent failures live.
