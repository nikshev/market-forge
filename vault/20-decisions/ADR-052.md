## Context

EXP-017 asks for twelve primary metrics per stop policy. Four of them —
maximum favourable excursion, maximum adverse excursion, holding time, and the
median and 95th-percentile stop distance — are properties of the *walk* from the
entry to the exit, not of the exit. [[REQ-WP-020]]'s replay had the path and the
position in hand and reported neither.

Computing them outside the replay means re-deriving the side convention: a
favourable excursion for a short is a fall, and a sign error there is invisible.
The numbers stay plausible and the favourable and adverse excursions simply
swap.

Extending the replay also surfaced a defect. `PricePoint` has carried
`data_quality_ok` since the replay was written, `StopPolicy.propose` has always
accepted it, and the replay never passed one to the other. PRD §44A.18's
data-quality freeze therefore could not fire on any replayed path — in the one
place a counterfactual evaluation is supposed to be able to show it.

## Decision

**The replay records the walk**, on both its loops, and `StopPolicyOutcome`
carries it: `mfe_r`, `mae_r`, `exit_at_ns`, `holding_ns`, `stop_distances_r` and
`points_observed`. Every field has a default, so nothing that constructed the
outcome before has to change.

**The stop distances are a list, not a summary.** §44A asks for a median and a
95th percentile, and a quantile of a summary is not a quantile.

**A position that never stopped has no holding time.** `None`, not the last
observed instant — reporting one would make an unfinished path look like a
completed trade.

**The replay passes `data_quality_ok` through.** A field that exists on the
input, is read by the policy, and never travels between them is a gate that
cannot be observed, which by §0's own standard is a gate that is not there.

## Consequences

- **The excursion has one implementation**, inside the loop that knows the
  position's side, with a short-position test as its control.
- **EXP-017's four walk metrics come from the production replay**, not from a
  second pass over the path in a research module.
- **The freeze is now testable in replay**, and a test asserts every instant of
  a frozen path holds.
- **`StopPolicyOutcome` is wider.** It was already the replay's whole output;
  the alternative was a parallel record keyed by policy name, which is the same
  data with a join.
