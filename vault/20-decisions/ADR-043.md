## Context

[[ADR-023]] held REQ-WP-019 at `tested` on 2026-09-08, because two of the five
conditions the PRD sets for it were unmet:

4. direct baseline metrics exist;
5. derivative experiment can return `NO_EDGE` without blocking product
   completion.

The fourth needed §13A.13's direct supervised targets, which need labelled
point-in-time data — REQ-WP-017. The fifth needed §13A.11's GMDH derivative
hypothesis — REQ-WP-018. Neither existed, and Constitution Principle IV forbade
building them first: no ML or GMDH layer until deterministic baselines exist and
leakage tests pass, and the deterministic baseline was REQ-WP-019 itself.

That ordering has now run its course. REQ-WP-017 landed with §23.5A's Target E
labels, availability shifted to confirmation time. REQ-WP-018 landed with the
GMDH search, the baselines §23.6 requires and the comparison report. Both are
`implemented` and both are CI-green.

## Decision

**REQ-WP-019 advances to `implemented`.** `channelflow.turning` supplies the two
missing conditions: `run_direct_baseline` scores §23.5A's Target E fold by fold
against the no-skill base rate, and `run_derivative_experiment` runs §13A.11's
forward-path hypothesis end to end and returns `NO_EDGE` as a value
([[ADR-042]]).

**REQ-NRT-F advances off `draft`.** §13A.12's stability gate is Test F, and it
now has tests: presence rate, horizon IQR and turn-type agreement recorded on
every assessment, promoted or not, over a deterministic lattice ([[ADR-041]]).

ADR-023's decision is superseded on this point and stands as the record of why
the work waited. Its reasoning was not wrong; its condition was met.

## Consequences

- **All twenty work packages are `implemented`.** The dashboard no longer shows
  one held below, and the reason it used to is the two commits that lifted it.
- **The last of the six `REQ-NRT-*` constraints leaves `draft`.** R5 has never
  been able to fire on REQ-NRT-F because nothing produced it a test.
- **Three of §13A.12's eight conditions are still deferred**, by name, in every
  promotion decision: extrapolation bounds, data-quality state and the
  walk-forward promotion gate. They need subsystems outside this feature, and
  naming them in the decision is what keeps a partial gate from reading as a
  complete one — the same device §23.6's unrun baselines use.
- **`implemented` is not `verified`.** Nothing here has run against a live venue,
  and the experiment's verdict on real data is unknown. The criterion was that it
  *can* return `NO_EDGE`, and it can.
