## Context

PRD §27.5 defines two ways to look at a channel:

- `AS-SEEN-THEN` — the immutable channel snapshot at the selected time;
- `CURRENT REFIT` — the channel calculated now over current history.

It calls the feature critical, sets `AS-SEEN-THEN` as the deep-link default,
and says it "directly exposes repaint-like differences and protects research
integrity". PRD §28.3 repeats the default at the API level.

The two views of the same instant can differ substantially, and the difference
is not a bug — it is the measurement. A channel refitted with a week of
hindsight will often look like it predicted what followed.

## Decision

1. **`as_seen_then` defaults to true** at the API and in the chart, including
   when the parameter is absent or unparseable.
2. **The chart always states which mode it is showing**, on screen, not in a
   tooltip or a settings panel.
3. **A refit that lacks history is refused**, never approximated over fewer
   bars.

## Consequences

- **A default that drifted to false would turn every deep link into a refit**,
  silently. Every alert this system has ever sent carries such a link
  ([[REQ-WP-008]]), so the failure would be retrospective as well as current:
  old signals would start looking better than they were.
- **An unlabelled chart is worse than either mode.** A reader who cannot tell
  which view they have will read a refit as evidence. The label is not
  decoration; it is the difference between a research tool and a way to fool
  yourself.
- **Refusing a short refit follows [[ADR-006]]'s reasoning at REQ-WP-006**: a
  channel fitted on fewer points than asked for is a different model wearing
  the same name, and here it would be displayed beside a real one.
- **The two modes must be testable against each other.** The success criterion
  is not "both work" but "they differ where they should, and the stored one is
  byte-identical to what was stored" — which is only checkable because
  REQ-WP-006's snapshots are frozen.
- Not reversible in any sense worth having: reversing it is the repainting the
  product exists to prevent.
