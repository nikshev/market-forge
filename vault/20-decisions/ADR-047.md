## Context

PRD §13.4's baseline C fits conditional quantiles directly — q10, q50, q90 — and
lists four required checks, one of which is "numerical stability".

Linear quantile regression is normally solved by linear programming, which here
would mean adding SciPy or statsmodels for one function. The obvious way to
avoid that is gradient descent on the pinball loss, and that is what this module
did first.

It was wrong in a way the acceptance tests did not catch. The pinball
subgradient is `tau` or `tau - 1` however small the residual — it never
vanishes — so a fixed step oscillates around the solution for ever. On a
perfectly flat series that oscillation *was* the channel: the three quantile
lines drifted apart and the model reported a 2.5% band on a series that never
moved. Smoothing the gradient near zero made it worse: the smoothing turns the
loss locally quadratic with curvature `1 / smoothing`, and a step of 0.5 against
a curvature of 10,000 diverges.

The mutation sweep is what surfaced it. "The minimum width is not enforced"
survived, because the spurious 2.5% band was already wider than the floor.

## Decision

**Enumerate.** A linear quantile regression's optimum passes through at least
two of the data points — the pinball loss is piecewise linear in the two
coefficients, so its minimum sits at a vertex. At a lookback of sixty that is
1,770 candidate lines, evaluated as one matrix in about two milliseconds.

Ties are broken on the coefficients — lowest loss, then lowest slope, then
lowest intercept — rather than by whichever pair the enumeration reached first.
A channel that is a function of a loop's index order is not a market fact.

## Consequences

- **Three research defaults disappear**: step size, smoothing width and
  iteration count. None of them had a principle behind it, and each was a number
  a reader would have had to trust.
- **Exact means reproducible without argument.** Principle XI is satisfied by
  construction rather than by a fixed iteration count.
- **It is O(n²) in the lookback.** At 60 bars that is nothing; at 5,000 it would
  be 12.5 million candidates and this decision would need revisiting. The cost is
  quadratic in a number the caller sets and can see.
- **PRD §0.14 is the ordering this follows**: correctness before performance
  optimization. The exact fit is both, at this size.
- **A flat series now returns the configured minimum width**, which is what
  §13.4's second required check exists to produce.
