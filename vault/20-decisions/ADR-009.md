## Context

REQ-WP-010 asks for a virtual clock, bar replay, strategy reuse and reports.
PRD §25.5 then lists twenty metrics a backtest should produce per setup: counts
and alert rates, but also win rate, expectancy in R, Sharpe, Sortino, maximum
drawdown, MFE, MAE, fees, slippage, funding cost and DEX gas.

Almost all of those need two things this feature does not have. Returns need an
outcome definition — PRD §40 defines what counts as a win, a loss and a target
hit — and they need a fill model, which §25.4 specifies separately. Neither is
built.

PRD §41 rule 9 states plainly: "Fees/slippage must be included in economic
evaluation."

## Decision

**Backtest v1 reports signal-quality metrics only**: candidate counts by family
and direction, state-path statistics, confirmation rate, and the distribution of
terminal reasons — invalidated, expired, resolved.

**It reports no returns, no win rate and no expectancy.** Those arrive with the
outcome definitions of §40 and the fill models of §25.4, together with the costs
§41 rule 9 requires.

## Superseded

Superseded on the returns question by [[ADR-048]] (2026-09-09), on the condition
this ADR itself named: PRD §40's outcome definitions, §25.4's fill models and a
cost model all exist now, and `economic_report` refuses to run without the last
of them. The signal-quality report below is unchanged, and still carries no
returns.

## Consequences

- **A return computed without costs would be a lie with a number attached.**
  §41 rule 9 exists because gross returns flatter every strategy, and a backtest
  that reports 62% win rate before fees will be quoted as 62%. Reporting nothing
  is honest; reporting a gross figure with a caveat is not, because the caveat
  does not travel with the number.
- **Signal-quality metrics are genuinely useful on their own.** How many
  candidates a configuration produces, how many survive to confirmation, and why
  the rest died, is exactly what tuning zone bounds and quality thresholds needs
  — and none of it requires knowing what happened to price afterwards.
- **The report will look thin next to §25.5's list.** That is the point: the
  gap between what is reported and what §25.5 wants is the visible measure of
  what is still unbuilt. Filling it with computable-but-meaningless numbers
  would hide that.
- **Strategy reuse is testable by parity rather than by inspection.** §25.2 says
  to avoid a separate backtest implementation of strategy logic. Rather than
  assert that by reading the code, the backtest is checked against the live
  engine driven directly over the same bars: identical candidate histories, or
  the two implementations have diverged. That is §35.5's parity requirement
  applied to the only components that exist yet.
- Reversible: adding returns later adds metrics to a report, not a second engine.
